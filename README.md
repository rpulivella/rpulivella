### Richard Ulivella

I lead the teams that transform hard professional workflows into software people actually want to use.

I have spent twenty-four years across film production and creative software, half of it leading product and technology. I build teams of passionate learners, set the tone, inspire ownership, and protect standards under pressure. Lead the team, not the tasks.

At Creamsource I led Slyyd, which pioneered app-first lighting control and won the 2026 HPA Award for Innovation in Production & Capture. I designed the agentic AI engineering practice and led the team through the transition, the first and only unit in the company to make it. Time between App Store releases went from four months to five weeks with defect rate flat, and one engineer covered four technology stacks at senior level. The harness types the code. I author the architecture, the semantics, and every kept-or-cut decision. Even 100% human-made software can ship as slop. The cause is rarely a lack of effort.

Before that I co-founded Lighticians and invented the technology that became Slyyd, as named lead inventor on US Patent 9,961,738 B2. Creamsource acquired it in 2022 and brought me on to lead product, technology, and design. Professionals in 60+ countries now use it on productions at Netflix, Apple, Amazon, Hulu, and Warner Bros Discovery.

For two decades I was a gaffer and lighting director on productions such as *The Crowded Room*, *You*, *Can You Ever Forgive Me?*, *Younger*, and *Pitch Perfect*, running departments of 20 to 50 under fixed deadlines.

I am a compulsive builder, continuously experimenting, and happy about it, especially when working outside of my comfort zones.

Shipping fast is cheap. Holding a standard while a whole team moves at AI speed is the new frontier where I work.

**About this profile.** Most of what I build ships inside a product or stays private. What lands here is the occasional tool that proved useful enough to publish.
